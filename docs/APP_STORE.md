# App Store copy and screenshot direction

App name: **SubSense AI**

Subtitle: **Subscriptions & Savings**

Promotional text: **See your recurring spending clearly. Review low-use services, plan cancellations and explore what you could save—all with a private, local-first portfolio.**

## Description

Know what you're paying for. Find what you don't use. Keep more of your money.

SubSense AI gives your subscriptions a calmer home. Add streaming plans, software, memberships and recurring bills, then see how they fit into your life.

**See the whole picture**
Understand monthly and annual commitments, explore spending by category and forecast upcoming renewals. Different currencies stay separate, so your totals stay honest.

**Find room to save**
Run a local subscription audit using your entered costs and usage ratings. Review low-use tools, possible overlaps, recorded price increases and trials nearing their end. Transparent Value, Cancel and Health Scores explain their reasoning.

**Turn an insight into a plan**
Explore savings scenarios, build cancellation checklists and set reminders. After cancelling or downgrading with a provider, record the action and see its projected effect on future spending.

**Ask your local Copilot**
Get answers to supported questions about costs, usage, category overlap and renewals. The current Copilot uses on-device rules, not a remote generative AI service.

**Your data stays yours**
No bank login. No email inbox connection. Your portfolio stays on your device. Import receipt text or a screenshot, review extracted details before saving, and export or delete your records whenever you need to.

Free includes up to five subscriptions, basic insights and renewal reminders. SubSense Pro adds unlimited subscriptions, audits, scoring, the savings simulator, local Copilot, advanced insights, household profiles and exports. Prices and renewal terms are shown before purchase. Export is also available before data deletion without Pro.

SubSense cannot cancel subscriptions for you. Costs and savings are estimates based on your entries, not financial advice. Household profiles are local labels; cross-device sharing is not included. Notification delivery depends on iOS permission, your dates and periodic app use.

## Seven screenshot panels

Use the native app with `--demo` and the demo banner visible. Fixture prices are illustrative. Capture real screens after the Apple build passes; do not substitute mock screenshots for functionality.

1. **See every subscription in one place** — Home dashboard; dark forest spend card, cream canvas, pale mint audit CTA.
2. **Discover money you could be wasting** — Run Audit; scroll to low-use findings and projected annual savings.
3. **Know what to review first** — Creative Cloud detail; Cancel Score and expanded explanation.
4. **Never miss a renewal reminder** — Calendar month with upcoming charges. Avoid an absolute delivery guarantee in supporting copy.
5. **Compare your next move** — Alternatives screen; show the verified-price input. Do not claim a live cheaper-plan search.
6. **Ask your subscription copilot** — Ask “Where can I save money?”; keep the local provider disclosure visible.
7. **See your progress take shape** — Record a demo cancellation, then Insights savings goal. Label values “projected reductions.”
