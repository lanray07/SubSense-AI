Subscriptions & Savings

Make room for what matters.

See recurring spending clearly. Review your subscriptions, plan renewals and explore potential savings with a portfolio stored on your device.

Your subscriptions, in one place

Add bills, memberships and software plans. See monthly and annual commitments, upcoming renewals and category breakdowns. Different currencies stay separate.

Turn a review into a plan

Use local audits, usage ratings and savings scenarios to decide what to keep. Create cancellation checklists and reminders, then record changes after arranging them with your provider.

Private local processing

Receipt parsing, screenshot text recognition and supported Copilot responses run locally. The Copilot uses deterministic rules. There is no bank connection, inbox access, cloud sync or remote generative AI service.

Free and Pro

Free includes up to five subscriptions, basic insights and reminders. Pro adds unlimited records, audits, scoring, savings tools, local Copilot, advanced insights, household labels and exports. Monthly and annual subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled through Apple. Prices and terms appear before purchase.

SubSense AI is in preparation for App Store release. It cannot cancel provider subscriptions. Costs and savings are estimates based on your entries. Household labels do not provide shared accounts.
