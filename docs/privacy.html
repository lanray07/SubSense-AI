Privacy policy

Effective 9 September 2026. This policy describes the current SubSense AI iPhone and iPad app and this support website. SubSense AI is maintained by the lanray07 project owner.

Data in the app

SubSense stores information you enter—subscription names, amounts, billing dates, usage ratings, notes, optional profile and income information, household labels, savings plans and recorded actions—on your device. The developer does not receive this portfolio data or operate a server that collects it.

Local processing

Audits, scoring, receipt text parsing, screenshot text recognition and supported Copilot responses run locally. Imported receipt details are presented for review before saving. The Copilot uses on-device rules, not an external generative AI service. The current app does not connect to your bank, read your email inbox, synchronise data through a developer service or monitor usage of other apps.

Permissions and security

Notifications are optional and use your entered dates. You can hide names and amounts in notification content. The optional app lock uses Apple's device authentication; the app does not receive your face data or passcode. The local portfolio uses iOS Data Protection. You control notification permission and device security settings.

Purchases and Apple services

Apple handles SubSense Pro purchases, billing, refunds and subscription management under its own privacy policy. The app uses StoreKit transaction and entitlement information to determine access to Pro. The developer does not receive payment card details through the app. Device backups may include local app data depending on your Apple settings; manage those backups with Apple.

No tracking or advertising SDKs

The current app includes no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, cross-app tracking or external AI upload. The developer does not sell app portfolio data.

Exports, deletion and retention

Your portfolio remains on your device until you remove it. Settings → Delete All Data removes the saved portfolio and pending reminders. Export is available before deletion without Pro. Exported files can contain personal financial information; only share them with recipients you choose. Files saved outside the app, copies shared with others, Apple purchase records and device backups are not removed by the app's deletion command. Deleting data does not cancel any subscription.

Support and this website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages and contains no added analytics scripts or advertising. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses for hosting and security under the GitHub privacy statement. If you contact support through a GitHub issue, the information you post is public and can be read by the developer and others. Do not post private records or credentials. Support correspondence remains in GitHub unless managed through its available controls.

Children

The app does not have accounts or a developer-operated data collection service, including for children. Do not submit children's personal information in public support requests.

Questions and changes

For privacy questions, contact the maintainer through the support page using a general description without personal records. The developer cannot access your local portfolio; use the app and device controls to export or delete it. This page will be updated if the app's data practices change, with a revised effective date.
