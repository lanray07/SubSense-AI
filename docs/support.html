How can we help?

For product questions, bugs or feedback, contact the project maintainer through GitHub.

Contact support on GitHub

GitHub sign-in is required to post. Issues are public: do not include financial records, receipt images, passwords, payment details or other private information. Describe the problem using fictional examples, your app version, iOS version and device model.

Getting started

Complete onboarding and add a subscription manually, or explore the separate demo portfolio. Review dates and amounts before saving imported receipt details. SubSense does not discover your subscriptions automatically.

Purchases and restoration

Open the Pro screen to view current monthly and annual plans. Use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used for your original purchase. If plans are unavailable, check your connection and try again.

Cancel a Pro subscription

On your device, open Settings, tap your Apple Account name, then Subscriptions and select SubSense Pro. Apple manages payment, cancellation and refunds. Deleting SubSense data or uninstalling the app does not cancel your subscription.

Cancel another service

Use the provider's own website or subscription settings. SubSense can help you plan and record the action, but does not perform cancellations for you.

Renewal reminders

Allow notifications in iOS Settings and check the dates entered in SubSense. Open the app periodically to refresh upcoming reminders. Notification delivery depends on device settings and permissions.

Export or delete your records

Settings offers reports and data controls. Delete All Data removes the local portfolio and pending reminders. Export is available before deletion without Pro. Copies you exported or shared, Apple purchase records and device backups are managed separately.

Privacy questions

Read the privacy policy. The developer cannot access or recover your local portfolio. For general privacy questions, use the support link above without posting personal records.
