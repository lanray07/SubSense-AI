# SubSense AI — premium artwork

Three coordinated promotional images generated with built-in Codex imagegen, using the app's forest, mint and ivory palette. Original PNGs are preserved. Reviewed for composition, text accuracy and absence of invented app UI.

| File | Dimensions | Intended use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| subsense-pro-artwork.png | 1254 × 1254 | Pro subscription artwork master; export to 1024 × 1024 before uploading to the optional subscription-image field. |
| subsense-hero.png | 1536 × 1024 | Website or campaign hero; left side provides space for live headline text. |
| subsense-campaign-poster.png | 1024 × 1536 | Portrait promotional poster with the headline “Make room for what matters.” |

Open index.html to review the set. The exact generation prompts are in PROMPTS.md.

These assets are marketing illustrations. They must not replace actual app screenshots or subscription review screenshots. No app icon was replaced and no App Store asset was uploaded. The square master is not yet a dimension-compliant 1024 × 1024 subscription export. Preserve the composition when resizing it.
